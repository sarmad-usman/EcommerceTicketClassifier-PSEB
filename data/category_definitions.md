# Category definitions

| Category | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Billing and Payment | Charges, invoices, payment failures, receipts and payment questions. | The customer reports a duplicate card charge for one purchase. |
| Order Status | Order confirmation, processing state and order progress questions. | The customer wants to know whether order 4821 has been confirmed. |
| Delivery Issues | Late, misrouted, missed or tracking-related delivery problems. | The parcel has not arrived by the promised delivery date. |
| Returns and Refunds | Returning an item and the progress or method of a refund. | The customer wants to return shoes that do not fit. |
| Account Access | Login, password, verification and account access problems. | The customer cannot sign in because the password reset link expired. |
| Technical Issues | Website or application errors, crashes, loading and functionality defects. | The mobile application closes whenever the customer opens the cart. |
| Product Information | Questions about product features, size, availability, specifications or warranty. | The customer asks whether the jacket is available in medium size. |
| Cancellation Requests | Requests to stop or cancel an order or subscription. | The customer wants to cancel an order placed ten minutes ago. |
| Damaged or Missing Items | Items received damaged, defective, incorrect or missing from a parcel. | The delivered mug arrived with a large crack. |
| Other Inquiries | General questions that do not fit the other nine categories. | The customer wants to know the store opening hours. |
